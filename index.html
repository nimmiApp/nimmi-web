The problem

Cash and cards both cost you.

Cash

Cash is slow and expensive.

Counting, cage queues, cash in transit and AML checks on every large buy-in.

Cards

Cards add fees and chargebacks.

Card fees come off every transaction, and disputes land back on you weeks later.

KYC

KYC and source of funds are a paper chase.

Checking ID at the door, then proving where a guest's money came from, means photocopies, bank statements and records kept by hand.
